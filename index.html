THE TONING & SCULPT GUIDE

Designed specifically to sculpt, tone, and define your body for a lean beach aesthetic. Focused on full-body circuits, core engagement, glute activation, and lean endurance without adding dense muscle bulk.

01. TONING BLUEPRINT

PHILOSOPHY: 3 full-body training sessions per week. Focus on light to moderate weights with high control and moderate-to-high rep ranges (10–15 reps).

CIRCUIT & SET STRUCTURE:

  • Set 1: 12–15 reps (Moderate Weight - Focus on Form).
  • Set 2: 10–12 reps (Slightly Higher Weight or Slower Tempo).
  • Set 3: 12–15 reps (Burnout / Tone Focus).
  • Rest Period: Keep rest between 60 to 90 seconds to maximize calorie burn.
02. LEAN NUTRITION BLUEPRINT

Keep your nutrition simple and lean to tighten up and achieve a smooth, island-ready tone:

• Daily Calorie Target: Goal Bodyweight (lbs) × 13

• Daily Protein Target: Goal Bodyweight (lbs) × 0.7g

*Note: Use your realistic goal bodyweight for calculated daily targets.

NUTRITION TIPS:

  • Tracking App: Log meals daily using Cronometer or MyFitnessPal.
  • Hydration Focus: Drink 2–3 liters of water per day to reduce water retention and keep skin glowing.
03. TONING PHASES

• Weeks 1–6 (Lean & Tighten): Focus on steady calorie deficit and building muscle tone.

• Weeks 7–12 (Island Sculpt & Core): Add higher rep endurance and targeted glute/core supersets.
