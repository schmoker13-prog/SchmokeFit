00. BAHAMA MAMA PROTOCOL
THE FEMALE SCULPT GUIDE

Tailored specifically for full-body toning, glute development, and core stability, this protocol focuses on maximum efficiency, sculpt-focused key movements, and flexible nutrition without endless cardio.

01. TRAINING BLUEPRINT

PHILOSOPHY: 3 full-body sessions per week, targeting glutes, upper body posture, legs, and core. Rest 1 full day between workouts for ultimate recovery.

REVERSE PYRAMID / PYRAMID TRAINING:

  • Set 1 (Heavy/Top Set): Target 8–10 reps (Challenging Weight).
  • Set 2 (Medium): Drop weight ~10%, target 10–12 reps.
  • Set 3 (Light/Burnout): Drop weight another ~10%, target 12–15 reps.
  • Rest Period: Rest 2 minutes between main sets; 60–90 seconds for supersets.

⏱️ TIME CRUNCH RULE: Short on time? Complete the Key Glute & Upper Body lifts for the day and skip optional isolation work.

02. FEMALE NUTRITION BLUEPRINT

Sculpting lean muscle while staying lean comes down to Calories and adequate Protein.

• Daily Calorie Target (Fat Loss): Bodyweight (lbs) × 12

• Daily Protein Target: Bodyweight (lbs) × 0.75g – 0.82g

*Note: Use target bodyweight if leaning down significantly.

TRACKING & FLEXIBLE EATING:

  • Tracking App: Log meals using Cronometer, MyFitnessPal, or Lose It!.
  • Hydration First: Aim for 2.5–3 liters of water daily for skin health and recovery.
  • Flexible Nutrition: Enjoy balanced meals without extreme restriction. 80% whole foods, 20% fun foods!
03. PHASE PROGRESSION

• Weeks 1–10 (Tone & Trim Deficit): Maintain a modest caloric deficit while building target muscle groups.

• Weeks 11–18 (Body Recomp): Slowly add 150 calories daily to shift into maintenance/toning phase.

• Reset & Repeat: Recalculate macros at your new target weight and continue progressing!

OFF DAYS & RECOVERY

Recovery is where shape and curves are built. Enjoy active recovery like light walking, mobility, or relaxing on rest days.
